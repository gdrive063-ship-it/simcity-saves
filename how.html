How to import a save

Follow these three steps to load any community city into Vij.

  1. 1

    Download the file

    Open any city on SimCity Saves and click the Download button to save the .json file to your device.

  2. 2

    Open Vij

    Launch the Vij city simulator on your computer or browser and head to the main menu.

  3. 3

    Choose Import

    Select Import in Vij and pick the downloaded .json file to load the city.

Saves are shared publicly. Do not put personal information in the title or description.
